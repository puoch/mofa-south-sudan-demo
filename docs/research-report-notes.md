# How the Deep Research report is used

Source: "Deep Research Report: About South Sudan for the MoFA WEBSITE"
(supplied 24 October 2026). It is an editorial and factual specification,
not approved content. Nothing from it is published in Phases 1 and 2.

## Adopted in the structure
- **Gateway with child pages.** The approved concept uses a short gateway
  with seven child pages; the report notes this is closer to the Serbian
  site's real structure.
- **Dated context notice.** The report recommends keeping time-sensitive
  political information in a separately dated, owned component. This is the
  `context-notice` component and the "Dated context notice" fields on the
  Country profile page type.
- **Per-fact status in quick facts.** Each fact carries a source and an
  approval status, so a pending figure (such as population) can never look
  approved.
- **Editorial metadata.** Last reviewed, content owner and sources on every
  page, as the report proposes.
- **No invented colours.** The report found no official HEX/RGB/CMYK/Pantone
  values for the flag; no national colours are defined.
- **Coat of arms as the primary government identifier**, sourced from an
  authorized master file and never redrawn.
- **Neutral imagery.** Hero images should be national landscapes or heritage,
  not political officeholders.
- **Accessibility guidance** (alt text for coat of arms, flag and maps; colour
  never the only signal) is reflected in the components and tests.

## Differences to resolve
- **Tagline.** The report suggests "Get to know South Sudan"; the approved
  direction and authorization use **"Discover South Sudan"**, which is what
  the gateway layout shows.
- **Child-page grouping.** The report proposes ten sections; the approved
  seven child pages group them differently (for example History and
  Government together). The approved seven are used.

## Held for approval (from the report)
Population figure, administrative structure wording, language wording,
current transition and election notice, post-2013 history, official map,
and all final copy.
