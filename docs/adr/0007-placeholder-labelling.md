# 0007. Every unverified item is visibly labelled

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-24

## Decision
- Every content type has an "Unverified placeholder content" checkbox, ticked
  by default. While ticked, the page shows a placeholder notice and teasers
  show a badge.
- Missions carry a "Jurisdiction status" (Unverified placeholder, Pending
  Ministry approval, Approved) that defaults to placeholder.
- Services carry a "Service status" that defaults to "Information only", so
  no service appears operational by accident.
- A site-wide banner states that the site is a development demonstration and
  not an official government website.
- Images use an "Authorized image pending" placeholder rather than stock or
  unlicensed photographs.
